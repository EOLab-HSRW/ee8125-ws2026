## How to get the material
Click the session link in the plan below (or in Moodle). It opens the JupyterHub and copies this repository into your
folder `~/ee8125-ws2026`, or updates it. **Your own changes are kept** when you click a link again — new or corrected
material arrives as new files.

## Exam
1. **Programming exam** (70 %): live in the PC pools in the exam phase.
2. **Project work** (30 %): groups of 1–2 students (join a group in Moodle), upload by **2027-02-28**.
   Rules and topics: `s10_Pandas_Time_Series/Project_Guidelines.md` (released in session 10).
